## Ishlash tartibi (har kadr uchun)
1. Flow'da avval RASM yarating (IMAGE PROMPT). Temur ishtirok etsa, reference sifatida
   Temur rasmlarini (№1 va №3) yuklang.
2. Rasmni "Video" bo'limida boshlang'ich kadr qilib, VIDEO PROMPT bilan klipga aylantiring.
3. Klipni K#.mp4 deb nomlab menga yuboring.
Bitta oddiy harakat. Chalkash sahna so'ramang.

## STYLE (har IMAGE PROMPTning oxiriga qo'shing)
Cinematic photorealistic historical drama, 14th-century Central Asia (Transoxiana), muted earth tones with warm firelight or golden hour, shallow depth of field, anamorphic lens, subtle film grain, historically accurate clothing and weapons (felt caps, wool robes, lamellar armor, recurve bows, curved sabers), no modern objects, no text, no watermark, vertical 9:16.

## TEMUR (Temur ko'ringan har promptning boshiga qo'shing)
The same man as in the reference images: TEMUR, a young man aged 24 to 30, strong brow ridge, wide cheekbones, firm jaw, short dark beard and mustache, black felt cap, dark grey-brown wool robe with a leather belt.
