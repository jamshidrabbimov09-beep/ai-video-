## Umumiy bloklar

**STYLE** (har promptning oxiriga):
`Cinematic photorealistic, 14th-century Central Asia, historically accurate clothing and weapons, shallow depth of field, film grain, no text, no modern objects, vertical 9:16.`

**TEMUR** (Temur ko'ringan promptda; Flow'da portret rasmi reference qilib yuklanadi, bizda bu i04):
`Temur (about 28, strong brow ridge, wide cheekbones, firm jaw, short dark beard and mustache, black felt cap, dark grey-brown wool robe with a leather belt)`

Flow "Agent instructions" ga ham shu ikki blok kiritilgan (Uslub, Temur).

## Rasmlarni tekshirishda topilgan xatolar (keyingi safar uchun)

- Zambarak g'ildiragi, chetli shlyapa (10-rasmning birinchi varianti): promptga taqiqlarni aniq yozish kerak.
- Yevropa o'rta asr naqshlari (ajdaho, grifon) qo'lyozmada.
- XVIII-XIX asr uslubidagi qilich dastasi (09), zamonaviy charm qo'lqop (07): qabul qilingan, mayda.
- Temur yoshi ~40 ko'rinadi (28 emas): barcha kadrlarda bir xil bo'lgani uchun qabul qilingan.
